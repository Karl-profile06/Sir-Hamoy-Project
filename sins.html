⚔ Seven Deadly Sins

Examine your heart daily and grow in virtue through God’s grace.

The Seven Deadly Sins lead the soul away from God. Through prayer, reflection, and virtue, we can overcome these weaknesses and grow closer to Christ.

👑 Pride

Meaning: Excessive love of self

Signs: Refusing correction, wanting recognition, thinking I am better than others

Virtue: Humility

Prayer: Lord Jesus, You humbled Yourself even unto death on the cross. Teach me true humility. Remove every form of pride in my heart, and help me to see myself as You see me — loved, but always dependent on Your grace. Let me serve others without seeking praise or recognition.

Challenge: Humbly accept correction today

💰 Greed

Meaning: Excessive desire for wealth or possessions

Signs: Never satisfied, fear of losing money, selfishness

Virtue: Charity

Prayer: Heavenly Father, everything I have comes from You. Free my heart from attachment to material things. Teach me to trust in Your providence and to be generous toward others, especially those in need. May I seek treasures in heaven, not on earth.

Challenge: Give or share something today

❤️‍🔥 Lust

Meaning: Disordered desire for pleasure

Signs: Impure thoughts, lack of self-control, objectifying others

Virtue: Chastity

Prayer: Lord Jesus, You call us to purity of heart. Cleanse my mind and body from every impure desire. Help me to see others with dignity and love, not with temptation. Strengthen me in moments of weakness and lead me to holiness.

Challenge: Avoid one temptation today

😠 Envy

Meaning: Resentment of others’ blessings

Signs: Jealousy, comparison, bitterness toward others

Virtue: Kindness

Prayer: Lord, forgive me when I compare myself to others. Teach me to rejoice in their blessings and to trust in Your perfect plan for my life. Remove envy from my heart and replace it with gratitude and love.

Challenge: Sincerely encourage someone today

🍽️ Gluttony

Meaning: Overindulgence in food or pleasure

Signs: Lack of self-control, excess in eating or drinking

Virtue: Temperance

Prayer: Lord, You have given me all that I need. Help me to practice self-control in my desires. Teach me to appreciate what I have without excess. May my body be used to glorify You, not satisfy uncontrolled desires.

Challenge: Eat with moderation today

🔥 Wrath

Meaning: Uncontrolled anger

Signs: Shouting, resentment, revenge thoughts

Virtue: Patience

Prayer: Lord Jesus, You were silent before Your accusers. Fill my heart with peace and patience. Help me to control my anger and respond with love instead of aggression. Heal the wounds in my heart that lead me to wrath.

Challenge: Stay calm in a difficult moment

😴 Sloth

Meaning: Laziness toward good works

Signs: Procrastination, spiritual laziness, lack of effort

Virtue: Diligence

Prayer: Lord, awaken my heart from spiritual laziness. Give me strength to do good even when I feel weak or tired. Help me to be faithful in small duties and to serve You with joy and perseverance.

Challenge: Complete one task you are delaying

Daily Examen Prayer

Give thanks for God's blessings today
Ask for light to see your sins
Review your day honestly
Ask forgiveness for sins
Resolve to grow in virtue
"Create in me a clean heart, O God" - Psalm 51:10
